# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)

### Fixed
- **A record without a birth date could bridge a same-name parent and child.**
  Scoring already sent such a pair to REVIEW (`exact_id_dob_mismatch`), but
  clustering only kept apart pairs with conflicting identifiers. A third record
  with no DOB that exact-matched both on a shared household email or phone joined
  all three. Found by project 02 (cdp-reference), where web form fills carry no
  DOB. Clustering now treats a DOB-mismatch pair (identical first names, both DOBs
  present and different) as a cannot-link, exactly like conflicting identifiers.
  A reviewer approving that exact pair lifts it; the conflicting-identifier rule
  still has no override. Splits list these pairs as `dob_mismatches`, and a
  "should be one entity" decision on a DOB-only split is reported as
  `refused_dob_rule`.

### Results
- No threshold, weight or config value changed. All six seeds were re-run,
  including the held-out seeds 2026, 99 and 31337, because the engine changed.
  The held-out results are **re-reported after an engine change, not tuned**:
  the fix was specified from a project-02 failure before any 01 numbers were
  regenerated, and no parameter was adjusted after seeing them.
- Every number in README.md and docs/results.md was regenerated by `make all`;
  see the diff of those files in this release's commit. In short, the headline
  mean pairwise precision moved from 0.9998 to 0.9999 and ordinary recall stayed
  at 0.8698. Every DOB cannot-link on all six seeds separated a true same-name
  parent and child; none split a true duplicate pair.
- The exact-ID policy comparison changed more than the shipped policy did: with
  the cannot-link in clustering, the "original" policy's Jr./Sr. MATCHes no
  longer reach the clusters. The shipped policy (`refined`) is unchanged. Any
  policy change would be a separate, pre-registered decision, because these
  numbers include the held-out seeds.

### Upgrading
